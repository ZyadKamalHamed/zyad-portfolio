SafeSize Triage

SafeSize 3D foot scanners sit on shop floors across Asia-Pacific. When one breaks, the fix used to start with a phone call. SafeSize Triage replaces that: machines report their own faults, store staff report through an AI chatbot, and every fault becomes a ticket routed to the right support tier across three countries.

Scoped and designed end to end, from discovery and data model to ticket lifecycle and 21 prototype screens, with a deployed Postgres schema where row-level security, not application code, decides who sees what. Every region choice keeps the data in Australia.

Next.js · Supabase Postgres · Claude on AWS Bedrock · Twilio

brand ↓ retailer ↓ store ↓ machine ↓ ticket

Two fault paths, machine telemetry and human report, converge on one ticket. Stores never see tiers, countries, or the supply chain behind their fix.

JARVIS

A personal AI operating system. A scheduled agent writes the morning briefing, plans the day, and files the tasks; the dashboard renders it all as a cinematic HUD with a voice to match. It improves itself nightly, one small verified change at a time, and a stealth mode strips anything personal server-side before a byte leaves the machine.

Built deliberately heavy on agents and light on stack: one zero-dependency Node process, a folder of JSON owned by the agents, and a browser.

  • Voice in three engines
  • Nightly self-evolution
  • Server-side stealth mode
  • Spaced-repetition study coach
